Frequently asked questions

How much roof space do I need?

It depends on system size. As a rough guide, 1 kW needs around 80 to 100 sq ft of shadow-free area.

How much can I save?

Savings depend on your usage, system size and local tariff, typically 30% to 70% of your bill.

Is net metering available?

It is commonly offered across India. We help with the application to your electricity distribution company.

Who can install rooftop solar?

Homes, housing societies, shops, offices, factories, schools and hospitals can all go solar.
